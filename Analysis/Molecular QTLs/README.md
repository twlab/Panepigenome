# Enrichment of CpG-altering variants among molecular QTLs

This directory contains code for testing whether CpG-altering variants are enriched among molecular quantitative trait loci (QTLs), including methylation QTLs (mQTLs), expression QTLs (eQTLs) and splicing QTLs (sQTLs).

## Overview

Molecular QTL annotations are evaluated in HPRC2 lymphoblastoid cell lines (LCLs) and across 50 GTEx tissues.

The workflow includes:

- integration of HPRC2 and GTEx molecular QTL annotations;
- filtering of CpG-altering and background variants by allele frequency and variant class;
- overlap analysis between CpG-altering variants and molecular QTLs; and
- enrichment testing for mQTLs, eQTLs and sQTLs.

## Molecular QTL annotations

HPRC2 eQTL annotations are obtained from the accompanying study.

mQTL and sQTL annotations are obtained from the `rust-fastQTL` resource.

GTEx molecular QTL annotations are evaluated across 50 tissues.

## Variant filtering

Enrichment analyses are restricted to variants with:

```text
MAF > 1%
````

For HPRC2 LCL analyses, the following variant classes are included:

* SNVs;
* indels; and
* structural variants.

For GTEx eQTL analyses, both CpG-altering and background variants are restricted to:

* SNVs; and
* indels.

Background variants consist of other common variants from the corresponding variant set.

## Enrichment analysis

For each molecular QTL class, the proportion of CpG-altering variants overlapping QTL variants is compared with the corresponding proportion among background variants.

Enrichment is evaluated using Pearson’s chi-squared test.

Fold enrichment is calculated as:

```text
Fold enrichment =
proportion of CpG-altering variants overlapping a QTL class
/
proportion of background variants overlapping the same QTL class
```

Values greater than 1 indicate enrichment of CpG-altering variants among molecular QTLs.

## Input

Typical inputs include:

* CpG-altering variant annotations;
* background genetic variants;
* variant allele-frequency information;
* HPRC2 mQTL annotations;
* HPRC2 eQTL annotations;
* HPRC2 sQTL annotations; and
* GTEx molecular QTL annotations.

## Output

Outputs generated by these workflows include:

* counts of CpG-altering and background variants overlapping each QTL class;
* enrichment contingency tables;
* Pearson’s chi-squared test P values;
* fold-enrichment estimates; and
* tissue-specific molecular QTL enrichment summaries.

## Reproducibility

Scripts in this directory correspond to the molecular QTL enrichment analyses described in the manuscript.

Analysis-specific input files, allele-frequency thresholds, variant-class restrictions and statistical procedures are documented in the corresponding scripts and in the Methods of the manuscript.
