# Enrichment at loci under directional selection

This directory contains code for evaluating whether CpG-altering genetic variants are enriched at genomic loci showing evidence of directional selection.

## Overview

CpG-altering variants are defined as genetic variants that create or disrupt a CpG dinucleotide. The workflow compares the frequency of CpG-altering variants at loci under directional selection with that of eligible non-CpG-altering variants from the same HPRC2 variant set.

The workflow includes:

- harmonization of directional-selection loci to GRCh38 coordinates;
- identification of CpG-altering and non-CpG-altering variants;
- filtering of variants by minor allele frequency (MAF);
- matching of variants across genomic coordinates and alleles; and
- statistical testing for enrichment of CpG-altering variants at directional-selection loci.

## Directional-selection loci

Genomic loci showing evidence of directional selection are obtained from the dataset described in the manuscript.

When required, coordinates are converted from hg19 to GRCh38 using UCSC `liftOver` and the `hg19ToHg38.over.chain.gz` chain file.

Loci that cannot be uniquely mapped to GRCh38 are excluded from downstream analyses.

## CpG-altering variants

CpG-altering variants are defined as variants that create or disrupt a CpG dinucleotide.

Variants are restricted to those with:

```text
MAF > 1%
````

Eligible non-CpG-altering variants from the same HPRC2 variant set are used as the comparison set.

## Variant matching

Directional-selection variants and HPRC2 variants are harmonized according to:

* chromosome;
* genomic position;
* reference allele; and
* alternative allele.

Only variants with matching genomic coordinates and alleles after genome-build harmonization are retained for enrichment analysis.

## Enrichment analysis

Enrichment is assessed by comparing the proportion of CpG-altering variants among directional-selection loci with the corresponding proportion among eligible non-CpG-altering variants.

The resulting contingency table can be represented as:

```text
                              Directional-selection loci    Comparison variants
CpG-altering variants                  a                         b
Non-CpG-altering variants              c                         d
```

Enrichment is evaluated using Pearson’s chi-squared test.

A two-sided:

```text
P < 0.05
```

is considered statistically significant.

The enrichment ratio can be summarized as:

```text
Enrichment =
proportion of CpG-altering variants at directional-selection loci
/
proportion of CpG-altering variants in the comparison set
```

Values greater than 1 indicate enrichment of CpG-altering variants at loci under directional selection.

## Input

Typical inputs include:

* genomic loci showing evidence of directional selection;
* HPRC2 genetic variant calls;
* CpG-altering variant annotations;
* variant allele frequencies; and
* genome-build conversion files where required.

## Output

Outputs generated by this workflow include:

* GRCh38-harmonized directional-selection loci;
* matched HPRC2 variants;
* CpG-altering and non-CpG-altering variant counts;
* contingency tables used for enrichment testing;
* enrichment estimates; and
* Pearson’s chi-squared test P values.

## Software requirements

Major software and command-line utilities used in this workflow include:

* UCSC `liftOver`
* R
* standard Unix command-line utilities

The `hg19ToHg38.over.chain.gz` chain file is used when coordinate conversion from hg19 to GRCh38 is required.

## Reproducibility

Scripts in this directory correspond to the directional-selection enrichment analysis described in the manuscript.

Analysis-specific input files, filtering criteria and statistical procedures are documented in the corresponding scripts and in the Methods of the manuscript.
